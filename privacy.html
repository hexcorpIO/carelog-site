CareLog Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026 · Hexcorp

The short version: CareLog has no servers. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account, shared only with the people you invite. We cannot see, sell, or analyze your family's care data, because we never receive it.

Who we are

CareLog is made by Hexcorp ("we"). This policy explains what the CareLog iPhone app does with information, and what it does not do. If you have a question, contact us at benton@hexcorp.io.

Where your data lives

  • On your iPhone. The care log, medications, appointments, notes, photos, documents and settings are stored in the app on your device.
  • In your iCloud. If you are signed in to iCloud, the same data is synced through Apple's CloudKit to your private iCloud database, and to the shared iCloud databases of the family members you invite. This storage belongs to you and to them, under Apple's privacy policy and iCloud terms.
  • Nowhere else. We operate no servers and no databases. We have no technical means to access your data.

What the app collects

Nothing is transmitted to us. In the language of the App Store privacy label, CareLog collects no data: nothing is sent off your device to Hexcorp or to any third party. The information below is processed only on your iPhone and in your iCloud.

  • Care information you and your family enter: the care recipient's name, date of birth, conditions, allergies, medications and doses, vitals, symptoms and incidents, appointments and questions for the doctor, handoffs, to-dos, expenses and receipts, medical bills, legal document status, scanned documents, emergency contacts and insurance card photos.
  • Family members' names, roles and optional phone numbers, so the circle can see who did what and call each other.
  • Doctor visit recordings and transcripts, only if you choose to record a visit and confirm that everyone present agreed. Audio stays on the recording phone unless you turn on the per-visit option to sync it to your family's iCloud.
  • Paid-aide shift notes, kept in a separate share that shows the aide only instructions, today's medications, a checklist and emergency contacts, never the family log.

Sharing with your family

When you invite someone, Apple's CloudKit sharing gives their iCloud account access to your care circle. They see what you see; the organizer can change roles and remove access at any time. A share link is only as private as the people you send it to.

Features that process data on your iPhone

  • Apple Intelligence. Drafting a visit summary or reading a scanned bill uses Apple's on-device models. The text never leaves your iPhone. Every result is a draft that you confirm before anything is saved.
  • Speech transcription. Visit recordings are transcribed on your iPhone using Apple's on-device speech recognition.
  • Document scanning. Text in scanned bills and documents is recognized on your iPhone. Account, claim and policy numbers are masked to their last four digits before being stored.
  • Emergency sheet and widget. A summary of the emergency sheet is cached on your iPhone so it opens instantly and can appear in a Lock Screen widget. Unlocking is required by default; you can choose to show a limited version (name, allergies, one contact) without unlocking.
  • Notifications. Dose reminders, alerts and the daily summary are scheduled on your iPhone from your own data. No notification content passes through us.

Permissions the app asks for

Camera and photo library (receipts, documents, photos), microphone and speech recognition (visit recordings), Face ID or passcode (app lock and hand-off mode) and notifications. Each is optional and used only for the feature named when iOS asks.

Subscriptions

The CareLog Family plan is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. Apple shares only anonymous receipt information needed to confirm the subscription is active. A subscription record is also written to your care circle in iCloud so your family members benefit from it.

No tracking, no analytics, no third parties

CareLog contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and no third-party software development kits. We do not track you across apps or websites and we do not sell or share data with anyone.

Deleting your data

In the app, More → Privacy → Delete my data removes every circle you own from your iPhone and your iCloud, leaves any circle shared with you, and clears the app's settings and cached emergency sheet. Deleting the app removes its local data; data in iCloud can also be managed in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage. Because we hold no copies, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Health information

CareLog is a coordination tool for families, not a medical service, and it is not medical advice. It is not a HIPAA covered entity. Treat the data as sensitive: keep app lock on and invite only people you trust.

Children

CareLog is intended for adults coordinating care and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Material changes will also be noted in the app.
